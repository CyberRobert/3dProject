# Nightmare Station
## Developers: Steven To, Christian Cate, Kristine Alba, Robert Pickell

### Main Concept
You spawn in the last car of a train. The goal is to get back to your station to go home. You must traverse through all the train cars to unlock the driver's compartment, then you steer back to your station and go home.
At every stop, you leave the car and check the train station for any anomalies. Afterwards, you head back into the train, and a system window pops up asking if you found any anomalies. (possibly add a “What was the anomaly?” if answered yes)
If you answer yes, then…
If you answer no, then…

### Storyline
You fall asleep on a train and wake up completely abandoned. The TV turns on to tell you the train has automated stops, and you need to get to the front to head back home. But there is something strange about the train stations. If you fail at any point, go to the “game over” scene in which you run back to the train
